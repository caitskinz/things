Parliament Assassin Caught in the Act

Matthew Hitchings

December 5, 2026 • 4:34pm

Jeremy Gagnon, 32, has been arrested for attempting to murder the leader of the Act party, David Seymour.

Gagnon was apprehended this afternoon after Parliament security intercepted him attempting to enter the debating chambers with a knife.

"I watched him take the knife out of his jacket and yell 'This is for you Seymour!'", says Parliament Security Guard Calvin Millward, "That's how I knew he was going for David".

Officer Christopherson told media that while the incident was disturbing, Gagnon posed no real threat to Seymour.

"He tried to get him in the debating chamber, which everyone knows is empty, y'know since today is a Saturday", says Christopherson.

While no official statement has been made by Seymour, his office has assured Things that the MP is safe at home in Epsom.
